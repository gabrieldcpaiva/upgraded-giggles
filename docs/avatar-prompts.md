# Part 3 — The Six Avatars

Six image-generation prompts for the crest badges. Chibi, circular gold rim, dark plum velvet, speech bubble carrying the canon tagline. Paste each into your generator of choice; the app's **Crest Forge** has one-click copies.

Shared style: circular gold-rim crest badge with engraved filigree, deep plum velvet backdrop, soft gold rim light, bold sticker-style linework, luxurious secret-society poster aesthetic, centered 3/4 portrait, 1:1 square. Each crest ships with an `avatar` field in the app's `AGENTS` data — drop the PNG in and the emoji crest steps aside.
